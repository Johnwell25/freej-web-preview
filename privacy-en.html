The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, listings and chats — nothing is sold to advertisers.
  • Other members see your public profile and listings, never your phone number or email unless you share them.
  • We check every member’s Emirates ID once, then delete the ID images and keep only the result.
  • Analytics cookies are off until you say yes; you can change your mind any time.
  • Close your account and we delete your personal data, apart from records the law requires us to keep.

This summary helps you read the policy; the full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Freej is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], registered in [EMIRATE], United Arab Emirates (“Freej”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Questions go to privacy@freej.ae.

2. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number, password (stored hashed), profile photo and area.
  • Identity check: your Emirates ID details, checked through UAE PASS or by an ID verification provider using photos of your card and a selfie. We delete the images once the check passes and keep the result, your ID number and its expiry date.
  • Business details (business sellers only): trade name, licence number, issuing authority, expiry date and a copy of your licence.
  • Listings: photos, descriptions, prices, category and approximate location.
  • Messages and offers: the content of chats, offers and handover arrangements between members.
  • One-time codes: codes we send to your email and mobile number to confirm they are active and yours.
  • Device and usage: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and error logs, used for security and to keep the site working.
  • Reports: details you send when you report a listing or a member.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you list, search and message.
  • To verify that every buyer and seller is a real person, and that business sellers hold a valid licence.
  • To show your listings and public profile to other members.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and prohibited items, and to review reports.
  • To send service messages, such as new-message alerts and security notices.
  • With your consent, to measure which features help, using analytics cookies.

4. Why we are allowed to use it

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, to meet legal obligations, to protect members and the platform, and — for analytics and any marketing — on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We follow the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and any regulations issued under it.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other members: your display name, profile photo, area, listings and the messages you send them.
  • Service providers who run parts of Freej for us under contract: our marketplace platform (Sharetribe), UAE PASS or our ID verification provider, email and SMS delivery, hosting, error monitoring and, only with your consent, analytics. Full list: [PROVIDER LIST].
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data stored outside the UAE

Some providers store data outside the UAE, for example in the European Union. Where that happens we use contractual and technical safeguards so your data stays protected to the standard this policy describes. [CONFIRM COUNTRIES AND SAFEGUARDS]

7. How long we keep it, and closing your account

We keep your data while your account is open. ID images are deleted as soon as your identity check passes. When you close your account, we delete your profile, listings, messages and documents straight away, and your email, mobile number and name within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires: records of any payments to Freej for 5 years under the UAE Commercial Transactions Law, and data linked to an open report, dispute or request from the authorities until it is resolved.

8. Your rights

  • See the data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your data or limit how we use it.
  • Object to processing, including any marketing.
  • Move your data to another service in a common format.
  • Withdraw consent, such as for analytics cookies.

Email privacy@freej.ae and we will reply within [30] days. You can also complain to the UAE Data Office.

9. Security

All traffic to Freej is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, access to personal data is limited to staff who need it, and our secret keys never reach your browser.

10. Age limit

Freej is for people aged [18] and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has an account, report it to us.

11. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. Change your choice any time in Cookie settings.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or on the site before the change takes effect.
