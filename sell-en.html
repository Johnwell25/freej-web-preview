How verification works

We check every account against the same list. When every check passes, your account is verified straight away. If one fails, a person on our team looks at it and tells you what to fix.

  1. 1Email code. We send a 6-digit code to confirm your inbox is real.
  2. 2Mobile code. A second code by SMS to your UAE number.
  3. 3Emirates ID. Verify with UAE PASS, or scan your card and take a selfie.
  4. 4Business licence (businesses only). Trade name, licence number, expiry date and a copy of the licence.

Questions sellers ask

Short answers about listing limits, ID checks and how we protect your data. Anything else, email hello@freej.ae.

What happens after 10 listings?

Neighbours can keep up to 10 listings live for free. To add an 11th, register as a small business and upload your Dubai licence. Your existing listings stay up.

Why do you ask for my Emirates ID?

We require an Emirates ID from every buyer and seller for the safety of everyone on Freej. It means you always know you’re dealing with a real, verified person. We check your ID once, keep only the result and the expiry date, and delete the ID images as soon as the check passes.

How do you keep my data safe?

All the information we collect is stored securely and encrypted. Your phone number, email and ID details are never shown on your listings or profile; buyers see your first name and area only. If you close your account, we delete your data, apart from records the law requires us to keep.

Which licences do you accept?

A Dubai trade licence that covers selling online, a Dubai e-Trader licence, or a Dubai free-zone licence with e-commerce activity. The beta is open in Dubai only.

Can I sell food?

Not yet. Food isn’t sold on Freej during the beta. Cosmetics and perfumes are fine.
